Naidan

Description

Nai Dan (Milk Egg) is a snack made by blending milk and eggs into a soft, tender custard. It has a mild sweetness, a smooth jelly-like texture, and a clean dairy-egg aroma. The name comes from its two key ingredients, and it is easy to prepare at home.

Served chilled or warm, Nai Dan is often topped with honey and crushed nuts. Its creamy flavor and delicate bite make it a comforting dessert or a light street snack, appealing to both children and adults.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • eggs
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Whisk milk, eggs, and sugar together.
  2. Strain the mixture to remove bubbles.
  3. Pour the mixture into small molds.
  4. Steam until the custard is fully set.
  5. Chill and top with honey and crushed nuts.
